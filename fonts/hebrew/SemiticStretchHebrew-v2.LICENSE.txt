Second-generation build. Adds hand-tuned per-variant widening for
aleph, ayin, tet, tzade, qof, and the finals — 22 widenable letters
total, up from six in v1. Trigger character U+05C6 (Hebrew Punctuation
Nun Hafukha). Compatible with mkmk mark-stacking for Judeo-Arabic use.
