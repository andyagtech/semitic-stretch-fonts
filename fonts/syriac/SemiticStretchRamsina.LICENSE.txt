Notes:
Cursive East Syriac at UPM=2048. Derived from the SIL Global "Ramsina"
release (originally Sparksoft "Nohadra" family, 1997–2025). Reserved
Font Names per OFL §3: "Ramsina" and "SIL" (upstream), plus "Marcus"
and "Nohadra" (Sparksoft). This derivative uses none of them and ships
as "Semitic Stretch Ramsina".

Kashida-style widening on 4 letters via GSUB ligatures on U+070D SYRIAC
HARKLEAN ASTERISCUS:
  beth ܒ, dalath ܕ, rish ܪ, taw ܬ
16 pre-baked stretch variants per letter. Because Ramsina is cursive
(supports Syriac init/medi/fina joining), the widening applies to the
ISOLATED form only — for right-joining letters (dalath, rish) that
covers every use since they always break the joining run; for
dual-joining letters (beth, taw) that covers end-of-word occurrences.
